Koobal Search Engine v1.0.0

Predictive suggestions for the stock VAB/SPH parts search bar.
Full functional release: PartSearch icon scroll-recycle, Enter focus unlock, and stable history hover/dismiss.

Install: copy GameData/KoobalSearchEngine/ into your KSP GameData/ folder.
